# CNGX1009

## Two chart axes share one orientation

This warning is emitted when a `<cngx-chart>` contains more than one horizontal axis (`position="top"` or `"bottom"`) or more than one vertical axis (`position="left"` or `"right"`). Both `[cngxAxis]` and the decoration-free `[cngxAxisDomain]` count.

A chart has one x scale and one y scale. It builds `xScale` from the first projected axis on a horizontal edge and `yScale` from the first on a vertical edge, in content order. Every mark and every axis then maps values through those two scales. An extra axis still draws its line, and it still derives its tick values from its own `[domain]`, but it positions those ticks with the chart's scale, which comes from the other axis.

The chart renders and nothing throws, but the extra axis lies: its labels sit at positions computed from another domain. The typical cause is an attempt at a second value scale on the right side:

```html
<cngx-chart [data]="metrics()">
  <svg:g cngxAxis position="bottom" type="band" [domain]="months()"></svg:g>
  <svg:g cngxAxis position="left" [domain]="[0, 50000]"></svg:g>
  <!-- WARNING: second vertical axis; its [domain] is ignored by the scale -->
  <svg:g cngxAxis position="right" [domain]="[0, 100]"></svg:g>
  <svg:g cngxBar [accessor]="revenueOf"></svg:g>
  <svg:g cngxLine [accessor]="marginOf"></svg:g>
</cngx-chart>
```

### Fixing the warning

#### Option 1: Keep one axis per orientation

Mount one horizontal and one vertical axis, and give the vertical one a domain that covers every series plotted against it.

#### Option 2: Split the series into two charts

When two series need different value scales (revenue in currency, margin in percent), draw them in two charts that share the horizontal axis definition:

```html
<cngx-chart [data]="metrics()">
  <svg:g cngxAxis position="bottom" type="band" [domain]="months()"></svg:g>
  <svg:g cngxAxis position="left" [domain]="[0, 50000]"></svg:g>
  <svg:g cngxBar [accessor]="revenueOf"></svg:g>
</cngx-chart>

<cngx-chart [data]="metrics()">
  <svg:g cngxAxis position="bottom" type="band" [domain]="months()"></svg:g>
  <svg:g cngxAxis position="left" [domain]="[0, 100]"></svg:g>
  <svg:g cngxLine [accessor]="marginOf"></svg:g>
</cngx-chart>
```

Each chart then also gets its own summary and data table, which describes each series against its own unit.

### Debugging the warning

The message is fixed text and does not name the chart or the axes. Look for a chart with two `cngxAxis` or `cngxAxisDomain` elements on `top`/`bottom` or on `left`/`right`. The query includes axes nested deeper in the chart's content, not only direct children.

The check runs in development mode only, once per chart, after the first render. Axes added later, or a `position` changed after the first render, are not checked again.
