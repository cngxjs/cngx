# CNGX1009

## Two chart axes share one orientation

### Why

`<cngx-chart>` drives its x scale from the first horizontal axis and its y scale from the first vertical axis. Two or more axes sit on the same orientation (for example `top` and `bottom`), so the extra ones render their line and ticks, but their `[domain]` is ignored.

### Fix

Mount one horizontal and one vertical `<cngx-axis>` per chart. For a second scale, use a second chart.
