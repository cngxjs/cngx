# CNGX1055

## Stepper or tab add-on without its presenter

This error is thrown when one of the add-on directives of a stepper or a tab group is created without the presenter it drives. The add-ons are `[cngxStepperRouterSync]` and `[cngxStepperSwipeNav]` from `@cngx/common/stepper`, and `[cngxTabsFragmentSync]` and `[cngxTabsRouteSync]` from `@cngx/common/tabs`.

An add-on holds no step or tab state of its own. The router-sync directives read the active step or tab from the presenter and write it back from the URL; the swipe directive calls the presenter's next and previous navigation. The presenter is `[cngxStepper]` for a stepper and `[cngxTabGroup]` for tabs. The organisms carry it for you: `<cngx-stepper>` and the other `@cngx/ui` steppers, `<cngx-tab-group>` and `<cngx-tab-nav>`, plus the Material bridges `[cngxMatStepper]`, `[cngxMatTabs]` and `[cngxMatTabNav]`. The router-sync directives look for the presenter on their own element and its ancestors in the same template; `[cngxStepperSwipeNav]` also looks past the template. Without a presenter the add-on has nothing to sync or navigate, so the constructor throws instead of leaving a deep link that never works.

```html
<!-- ERROR: the router sync sits on a wrapper, not on the tab group -->
<section cngxTabsRouteSync>
  <cngx-tab-group aria-label="Account">...</cngx-tab-group>
</section>
```

### Fixing the error

Put the directive on the element that carries the presenter:

```html
<cngx-tab-group cngxTabsRouteSync aria-label="Account">...</cngx-tab-group>
```

```html
<cngx-stepper cngxStepperRouterSync>...</cngx-stepper>
```

`[cngxStepperSwipeNav]` is applied by `<cngx-stepper>` itself through its `[mobileSwipe]` input; you only place it yourself on a custom stepper skin, inside or on the element with `[cngxStepper]`.

### Debugging the error

The message names the directive and the presenter it needs, for example `CNGX1055: [cngxTabsRouteSync] must be placed on or inside an element with [cngxTabGroup], such as <cngx-tab-group> or <cngx-tab-nav>.` In a production build the message is the bare code `CNGX1055`; the check still runs there.

The check runs once, in the constructor. A router-sync directive looks at its own element and its ancestors within the template it is declared in. A presenter on a child element does not count, and neither does one in another component's template, for example a wrapper component that renders the `<cngx-tab-group>` around content you pass in. A missing `Router` is a different case: it is reported as the warning [CNGX1031](cngx1031.html), and the directive becomes a no-op.
