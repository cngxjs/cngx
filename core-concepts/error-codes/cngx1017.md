# CNGX1017

## Status reduced to a bare coloured dot

### Why

`<cngx-status>` has `[glyph]="false"` and no visible `[label]`. Without the glyph and the text, the tone is carried by colour alone, which a colour-blind reader cannot tell apart. An `aria-label` does not help here: it names the status for assistive technology, not for a sighted reader.

### Fix

Show a visible `[label]`, or leave `[glyph]` on so the tone has a shape as well as a colour.
