# CNGX1001

## Unknown earcon

### Why

The audio engine was asked to play an earcon name that is not registered. Nothing plays. The engine warns once per unknown name, so a later play of the same name stays silent in the console as well.

### Fix

Register the earcon before you play it, app-wide with `withEarcons({ ... })` in `provideCngxAudio(...)` or at runtime with `engine.register(name, config)`. Otherwise check the name for a typo against the registered set (the defaults plus your own).
