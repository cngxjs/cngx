# CNGX1039

## More than six steps at one level

This warning is emitted when a stepper has more than six steps and none of them is inside a step group.

A stepper strip shows every top-level step as a marker with a label. Past six steps the labels no longer fit at reasonable widths, the strip truncates or wraps, and users lose the overview that a stepper is supposed to give them: where they are, how far it is, what comes next. Screen-reader users hear "step 5 of 11" without a sense of structure.

`[cngxStepGroup]` groups related steps under one label. The strip shows the groups as headers, a group-collapse policy can fold the ones the user is not in, and the summary badge on a collapsed group keeps it informative.

```html
<!-- WARNING: seven steps, all at the top level -->
<cngx-stepper aria-label="Onboarding">
  <div cngxStep label="Profile">...</div>
  <div cngxStep label="Preferences">...</div>
  <div cngxStep label="Repository">...</div>
  <div cngxStep label="Pipeline">...</div>
  <div cngxStep label="Team">...</div>
  <div cngxStep label="Billing">...</div>
  <div cngxStep label="Review">...</div>
</cngx-stepper>
```

### Fixing the warning

#### Option 1: Group related steps

```html
<cngx-stepper aria-label="Onboarding">
  <div cngxStepGroup label="Account">
    <div cngxStep label="Profile">...</div>
    <div cngxStep label="Preferences">...</div>
  </div>
  <div cngxStepGroup label="Project">
    <div cngxStep label="Repository">...</div>
    <div cngxStep label="Pipeline">...</div>
    <div cngxStep label="Team">...</div>
  </div>
  <div cngxStep label="Billing">...</div>
  <div cngxStep label="Review">...</div>
</cngx-stepper>
```

One step inside any group is enough to silence the warning; an empty group does not count. Group by meaning, not to reach the threshold.

#### Option 2: Reduce the number of steps

Merge steps that each ask for one or two fields into one step, or move optional settings out of the flow.

### Debugging the warning

The message starts with `CNGX1039: [cngxStepper] more than 6 steps and none inside a [cngxStepGroup]`. It does not identify the instance; look for steppers with seven or more `cngxStep` elements and no `cngxStepGroup`.

The check runs in development mode only, once per stepper, after the first render, and counts the steps registered at that moment. Steps added later (from data that loads after the first render) are not counted, so a stepper can grow past six without the warning. It is advice, not an error: the stepper works the same with or without groups.
