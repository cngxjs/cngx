# CNGX3012

## Tab overflow could not find the tab strip

### Why

`<cngx-tab-overflow>` watches the tab strip of its `<cngx-tab-group>` to list the tabs that do not fit. It looks for the strip once per animation frame, for up to 60 frames, and never found it. The "More" popover stays empty, and clipped tabs are not reachable through it.

### Fix

Project `<cngx-tab-overflow>` inside `<cngx-tab-group>` through its content slot, not next to it. With Material tabs, use `[cngxMatTabs]`, which mounts the overflow itself.
