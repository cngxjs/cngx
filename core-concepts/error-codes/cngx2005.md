# CNGX2005

## cngxPasteTransform stacked on cngxInputMask

### Why

`cngxPasteTransform` and `cngxInputMask` both cancel the native paste and insert the text themselves. On one input they do it independently, so pasted text goes through two uncoordinated paths and is applied twice.

### Fix

Remove `cngxPasteTransform` from the masked input. Do per-character cleanup through the mask itself, with its `[transform]` input or `customTokens`.
